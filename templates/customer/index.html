Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about EGGIFY gym egg distribution.

Are these normal raw fresh eggs or boiled eggs?

EGGIFY supplies premium, farm-fresh normal eggs (unboiled). They are sourced directly from vetted poultry farms, graded, sanitized, and packed in secure crates so you can prepare them however you prefer.

How do I collect my order at the gym?

Once your payment is verified, you receive an Order ID and digital pickup pass. On your selected delivery date, fresh crates arrive at your gym's front desk. Simply mention your name or Order ID to the desk trainer and collect your crate!

What if an egg is cracked during transit?

We maintain strict quality control and pack a buffer reserve. If you encounter any broken eggs upon collection, report it via our WhatsApp support with your Order ID for an immediate replacement or refund.
